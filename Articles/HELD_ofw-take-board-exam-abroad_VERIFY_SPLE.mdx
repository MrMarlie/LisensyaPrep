# I'm an OFW Abroad — Can I Take the Philippine Board Exam from Overseas?

*By LisensyaPrep Team | Last Updated: July 2026*

---

**The direct answer: yes — the PRC conducts the Special Professional Licensure Examination (SPLE) for OFWs at overseas venues, historically across the Middle East and select Asian hubs, for a set of professions announced per year (the LET for teachers is the flagship). If your profession or location isn't covered in a given cycle, the fly-home route is the workable plan B — expensive, but routinely done with leave-and-logistics planning. Here's how both roads run.**

## The SPLE: the exam that comes to you

The PRC has run **Special Professional Licensure Examinations** for Filipinos working abroad for years — overseas administrations historically held in **Middle East hubs (Saudi Arabia's cities, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman) and at times select Asian centers**, typically in an annual window. Coverage is **per the year's official announcement**: the **LET has been the SPLE's flagship** (thousands of OFW teachers and would-be teachers take it abroad), with other professions included per cycle.

The permanent mechanics that hold across cycles: **application runs through [LERIS](https://lisensyaprep.com/blog/leris-prc-online-guide)** with SPLE-specific venue selection during the announced window; requirements mirror the domestic exam ([the same document set](https://lisensyaprep.com/tools/requirements-checker) — and note your documents are *Philippine* documents: PSA certificates and school records still come from home, so enlist family early for the gathering); slots at overseas venues are limited and fill fast; and Philippine embassies/consulates and POLO-DMW offices circulate the announcements — follow their pages from your host country.

## Reviewing from abroad (the quiet advantage)

OFW reviewees hold an underrated edge: the **online review era erased the geography problem**. Question banks, video lectures, and [phone-based reviewers](https://lisensyaprep.com/) work identically in Riyadh and Rizal; [the working-reviewee architecture](https://lisensyaprep.com/blog/board-exam-review-while-working) maps directly onto OFW shift life; and Gulf-based LET-SPLE study groups are a genuine institution — find yours through the Filipino community pages of your city. Time-zone tip for live review classes: many PH-based online programs schedule Middle-East-friendly sessions precisely because the SPLE market exists — ask before assuming the hours won't work.

## Plan B: the fly-home exam

If your profession or city isn't in the year's SPLE coverage, the fly-home route is standard practice: target the exam date from [the PRC calendar](https://lisensyaprep.com/blog/prc-board-exam-schedule), file leave far ahead (2-3 weeks ideal: arrival buffer, [the room-assignment visit](https://lisensyaprep.com/education/let-september-2026-guide), exam days, and the jet-lag tax — flying in 48 hours before a morning exam is self-sabotage), and budget it honestly ([airfare turns the exam's cost into its largest line](https://lisensyaprep.com/blog/board-exam-total-cost-budget)). Many OFWs pair the trip with annual vacation — the exam-cation is a well-worn OFW tradition.

## Why it's worth the trouble

The license earned mid-contract compounds every future move: [it's the credential your next abroad application reads](https://lisensyaprep.com/blog/is-working-abroad-worth-it), the [RA 1080 eligibility](https://lisensyaprep.com/civil-service/government-jobs-cse-eligibility) waiting for the balik-Pilipinas chapter, and — for the thousands of OFW household workers and cross-field workers taking the LET-SPLE — the profession that turns the abroad years into a launchpad instead of a loop. The exam coming to Riyadh exists because OFWs kept asking for exactly that chance. Take it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can OFWs take PRC board exams abroad?**
Yes — the SPLE brings selected board exams to overseas venues (historically Middle East hubs and select Asian centers) per the year's official PRC announcement, with the LET as the flagship.

**How do I apply for the SPLE?**
Through LERIS during the announced window, selecting the overseas venue — with Philippine-sourced documents (PSA, school records) gathered via family at home, and announcements tracked through embassies and POLO-DMW offices.

**What if my profession isn't in this year's SPLE?**
The fly-home route: target a domestic cycle, file 2-3 weeks' leave for buffer and jet lag, and budget airfare as the exam's largest cost line.
